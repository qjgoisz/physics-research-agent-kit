# Terminology, style, and referee review

## Terminology pass

Build three ledgers:

1. internal shorthand that must be replaced or explicitly defined;
2. legitimate technical terms that require a first-use definition for the expected readership;
3. canonical spelling, capitalization, hyphenation, and phrasing for contested terms.

For each section, inventory non-elementary terms and symbols. Confirm that each is previously defined or standard for the stated audience. Present doubtful items to the user rather than silently deciding they are universally understood.

## Style consistency pass

Check tense and voice by section, canonical recurring phrases, deprecated variants, and sentence habits such as stacked hedges, empty announcement sentences, repetitive connective openings, or vague emphasis. Consistency serves precision; do not flatten deliberate distinctions or the author's voice.

## Figures and notation

Require labeled axes and units, uncertainty information where relevant, self-contained captions, and text that says what evidence a figure provides. Check that notation, constants, units, transforms, and sign conventions match `RESEARCH-STATE.md`.

## Skeptical referee pass

List objections under:

- unsupported or over-broad claim;
- missing control or convergence evidence;
- plausible alternative explanation;
- possible numerical or plotting artifact;
- figure that does not establish the cited sentence;
- missing or misused prior work;
- reproducibility or disclosure gap.

For each objection, identify the affected claim, evidence, severity, and smallest adequate remedy. Separate resolved objections from those requiring user action or new research.
