# Venue and claim integrity

## Venue requirements

Use current official publisher or venue sources. Record the URL and date checked for structure, length limits, templates or class files, abstract rules, figure/table specifications, references, data/code availability, and AI-assistance disclosure. Label deadlines, fees, limits, template versions, and policies as volatile and recheck them before submission.

Do not derive venue rules from an old manuscript, a third-party summary, or memory when an official source is available. A venue-specific pack may assist, but current official rules remain authoritative.

## Citation verification

For every consequential citation:

1. resolve its DOI, arXiv identifier, or official bibliographic record;
2. inspect the relevant primary text where access permits;
3. record the exact claim supported and relevant assumptions;
4. weaken, qualify, replace, or flag the sentence when support is incomplete.

Existence and support are separate checks. Never attach a citation based only on title similarity or remembered reputation.

## Quantitative traceability

Map each number, scaling, trend, and physical conclusion to one of:

- a specific `Results.md` entry;
- a recorded derivation and its assumptions;
- a verified external source.

If no origin exists, compute or verify it within scope, or mark it unresolved and tell the user. Do not round, rephrase, or qualify a number into apparent agreement.

## Source use

Paraphrase and cite; do not copy source sentences. Quote only when exact wording matters and attribution is explicit. Follow the recorded venue policy for AI assistance and disclosures.
